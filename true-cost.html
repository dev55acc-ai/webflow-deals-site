Why it exists

We got the number wrong first

This site exists because the cost of a Webflow site is never just the template price. It's the plan that publishes it, the add-ons the design quietly requires, the second licence a client project forces, and the tools stacked on top. Add them up from a pricing page at midnight and you will usually get it wrong — in the direction that hurts.

So we built the sheet we wanted before we bought anything. Every figure in it is footnoted to the vendor's own pricing page with the date it was checked, so you can re-verify any row yourself instead of trusting ours. When a vendor changes a price, the check date is what tells you the row went stale.

How to use it

Ten minutes, then take it to the vendor

  • Open the summary table and sort by annual cost at your team size — not at one seat.
  • Check every tool you already pay for against its current pricing page using the check-date column as your prompt.
  • Flag anything where the per-user math, not the headline number, decides the tier.
  • Bring the rows to the renewal conversation. "Annual cost at 8 seats" is a different sentence from "what's your price."
