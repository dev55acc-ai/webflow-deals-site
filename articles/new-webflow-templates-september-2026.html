A $79–$99 template with a strong author track record is usually the sweet spot: cheap enough to be one project's cost, expensive enough that the author can afford to keep it updated. The $129 entries (Genesis Lab, Nivoro) tend to be large multi-page systems — worth it only if you'll actually use the extra pages, not just because more looks more valuable.

The free ones worth a look

Free templates are the fastest way to feel out the Webflow workflow without spending anything — you can run them in the free Starter plan and only pay when you connect a custom domain. Currently free on the marketplace: Lighter (Kanvas), Layout (Kedd), Finanex (Mezario), Studiores (Studio81), Fiilo (Gr8r Studio), Osvald (EightAM), Funnelra (Flowcub Design), and HelloBot (Saeculum Solutions). Studiosres (Studio81) is a recent free addition we'd single out — a flexible studio-layout worth importing just to study.

How to shortlist this list

  1. Open each candidate's live preview — the marketplace preview is the real site, not a mockup.
  2. Click into a secondary page (services, project, blog) — that's where design quality shows.
  3. Check the author's other templates and update history; a studio with several recent listings fixes bugs.
  4. Resize to mobile and scroll the hover-dependent menus.
  5. Then check the licensing footnote: one live project per purchase.
