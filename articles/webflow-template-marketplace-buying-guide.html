Webflow Template Marketplace Buying Guide

Before you spend $79–$149 on a template, understand licensing, CMS access, update rules, and the hidden costs that make a "cheap" purchase expensive.

WebflowDeals editorial · Published September 22, 2026 · ~2 min read

What the license actually covers

Every Webflow template purchase grants a single-site license. You get the design files, CMS collections (if included), and the ability to clone it into one project. If you need a second site — even a personal portfolio — you must buy the license again.

Key trap: buying a portfolio template that doesn't include CMS collections. You think you can reuse it for a client's site; the license says otherwise.

CMS access and export rules

Templates that include CMS collections keep their structure when cloned. You cannot export CMS data to another platform without manual work. This matters if you plan to migrate from Webflow later — the template choice locks you into the platform.

How to evaluate before buying

Check the live demo on desktop and mobile. Confirm the number of CMS items allowed by the template's collections. Read the author's update policy — some templates are abandoned after release.
